Good to know

Common questions.

Can't find your answer? Call us on 01621 733123.

Do I need a licence for two-way radios?

Licence-free radios are a cheaper option for amateur and leisure users. For business needs, licensed two-way radios work within a broad range of frequencies. Tell us how you will use them and we will advise.

Can you install and test the system on site?

Yes. Installation includes on-site setup, feature operation and coverage testing.

Which brand should I choose?

We advise on your requirement, not by brand, and we supply all major brands including Motorola, Kenwood, Hytera and Icom.

Do you ship across the UK?

Yes. Shipping is £8.95 per order and prices shown exclude VAT.

Can I hire radios instead of buying?

Yes. Get in touch with your dates and quantities and we will quote.
